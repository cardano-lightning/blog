Cardano Lightning

[Permissionless payments that are instant, cheap and ergonomic]

Problem

Bitcoin for payments

Bitcoin was touted as a permissionless payment layer for the world. However, for most day-to-day interactions it is too slow and/or too expensive.

Bitcoin Lightning

Bitcoin Lightning solved this.

By creating a second layer, users can transact instantly and with low fees without compromising permissionless-ness.

A key insight is that a Lightning network topology can reflect usage; contrast with L1s where everyone must tell everyone everything.

But

Solved in theory but in practice, Bitcoin Lightning has issues.

A fundamental issue is that in Bitcoin Lightning all nodes are homogeneous. Whether you just want to use lightning to buy coffee, or route $$$, you must maintain your infra. Or handover custody.

In addition, liquidity management is hard at scale.

Solution

Cardano Lightning: Make channels and nodes that fit user needs.

How

Cardano exposes primitives Bitcoin doesn’t. We can do things they can’t.

Decouple the L1 mechanics from the L2 messages.

Keep interop. Don’t compromise on permissionless.

Ergonomic

Normal users can their coffee with just their mobile app without compromising safety.

Simplify liquidity management and lower the barrier to be service provider without becoming a custodian.
